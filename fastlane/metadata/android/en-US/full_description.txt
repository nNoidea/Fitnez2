Fitnez2 is a fast, offline fitness tracking app specially crafted for weight exercises and gym workouts. Built with Material 3 Expressive design, large touch targets, and tactile haptic feedback so you can log your lifts with zero friction.

Features:
• 100% Offline & Private: No internet permission, no accounts, and no trackers. Your data never leaves your device.
• Material 3 Expressive UI: High-contrast, big buttons designed for effortless logging between sets.
• Rich Haptic Engine: Tactile clicks, snaps, and spring tension detents for confident physical feedback.
• Workout & Exercise Management: Group exercises into workouts or track individual lifts on the fly.
• Progress Tracking: Visualize your volume and progressive overload over time with built-in charts and calendar views.
• Backup & Restore: Simple JSON export and import using Android's Storage Access Framework.
